Full Specification & Modules

All Features

A complete, transparent index of all 24 capabilities and 7 core architectural modules built into MySoundLib.

01

Library Management

Multiple Libraries
Create and switch between distinct sound libraries for different clients, YouTube channels, projects, or general audio utility.
Export with Tags
Export entire libraries with all tags, groupings, and embedded metadata intact for complete backups or seamless migration to other machines.
Group Organization
Create internal folders/groups with flexible nested hierarchies (drag and move groups inside or outside other groups effortlessly).
Drag & Drop Import
Instantly add single audio files or entire folder directories by dragging them straight into the application interface.
View Modes
Toggle between a hierarchical tree view organized by groups/folders or a continuous flat list view of all indexed files.
02

File System & Sync

Local Sync
On-demand synchronization for designated disk directories. New files added to your local folder are indexed whenever you trigger sync, letting you keep standalone files or linked sync folders under total control.
Bidirectional Renaming
Rename files and groups inside the app with automatic, instant updates reflected in your synchronized disk folders.
Deletion Management
Choose to remove an audio file only from the library catalog index or send it safely to your operating system's Recycle Bin / Trash.
03

Tags & Metadata

Tag Management
Create, assign, and edit custom text tags for fast cataloging, indexing, and multi-tag filtering.
Type Categories
Define custom classification types for individual audio files, internal groups, and synchronized folders (e.g. SFX, Foley, Music, Ambience).
Metadata Display
Inspect technical file properties, duration, sample rate, bit depth, channel configuration, and embedded metadata tags directly.
04

Search & Filters

Multi-Factor Search
Search across filenames, tags, categories, types, and source folders at the same time.
Advanced Filters
Granular filtering by audio format (WAV, MP3, FLAC, OGG), assigned categories, and channel count (mono, stereo, multichannel).
Global Search Overlay
Press a hotkey over Premiere, DaVinci, or CapCut and a search bar pops up right over your screen. You search, preview on spacebar, and drag the sound straight into your timeline without switching windows.
05

Player & Audio Editing

Waveform Preview
Instant audio and waveform playback in the main app window and directly inside the floating search overlay with spacebar.
Precision Trimming
Select and extract specific transients, hits, or tail snippets non-destructively directly from the visual waveform.
Pitch Shifter
Real-time pitch adjustments to alter the tone and speed of the audio or trimmed selection before inserting it into your project.
Format Conversion
Direct, fast batch conversion between all supported audio formats with one-click export to clean broadcast WAV.
06

Workflow & Integration

Drag to Timeline
Drop the full audio track or trimmed waveform snippet directly onto your video editor or DAW timeline in 2 seconds flat.
Universal Compatibility
Native drag-and-drop support for Premiere, DaVinci Resolve, Final Cut Pro, CapCut, After Effects, Ableton, FL Studio, Reaper, and Vegas.
07

System & Platforms

Multiplatform Support
Native performance and identical feature parity across Windows, macOS (Intel & Apple Silicon), and Linux.
Customizable Shortcuts
Full hotkey customization for global overlay invocation, playback controls, and rapid editing workflows.
Advanced Settings
General software preferences to fine-tune your workflow, default behaviors, and customize usability.
Lightweight Background Engine
Lightweight execution in the background with minimal CPU and RAM usage, leaving your computer free to render and edit.
