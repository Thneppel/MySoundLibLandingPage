Feature Specification

All Features

A breakdown of all 24 features and 7 modules in MySoundLib.

01

Library Management

Multiple Libraries
Create and switch between distinct sound libraries for different clients, YouTube channels, projects, or general audio utility.
Export with Tags
Export entire libraries with tags, groups, and metadata for backups or moving to another machine.
Group Organization
Organize sounds into nested groups by dragging them inside or outside other folders.
Drag & Drop Import
Add individual audio files or entire folders by dragging them into the app.
View Modes
Switch between a folder tree view and a flat list of all indexed files.
02

File System & Sync

Local Sync
Syncs chosen folders on demand. New audio files in your local folders are indexed whenever you refresh.
Bidirectional Renaming
Rename files and groups inside the app to update their names in your synchronized disk folders.
Deletion Management
Choose whether to remove a file from the index or send it to your system Trash.
03

Tags & Metadata

Tag Management
Create, assign, and edit custom text tags for fast cataloging and multi-tag filtering.
Type Categories
Assign categories to audio files, groups, and folders (such as SFX, Foley, Music, or Ambience).
Metadata Display
View file duration, sample rate, bit depth, channel configuration, and embedded metadata tags.
04

Search & Filters

Multi-Factor Search
Search across filenames, tags, categories, and source folders at the same time.
Advanced Filters
Filter results by format (WAV, MP3, FLAC, OGG), category, and channel layout (mono, stereo, multichannel).
QuickSearch Overlay
Press Alt + Shift + F while editing in Premiere, DaVinci, or CapCut. Search, audition sounds instantly, and drag the clip into your timeline without switching windows.
05

Player & Audio Editing

Waveform Preview
Instant waveform rendering and audio playback in both the main window and QuickSearch Overlay.
Precision Trimming
Select and extract transients, impacts, or tails non-destructively directly on the waveform.
Pitch Shifter
Adjust audio pitch and speed before dragging the sound into your timeline.
Format Conversion
Batch convert between supported audio formats with one-click export to WAV.
06

Workflow & Integration

Drag to Timeline
Drag the full track or trimmed waveform snippet directly into your video editor or DAW timeline.
Universal Compatibility
Drag-and-drop support for Premiere, DaVinci Resolve, Final Cut Pro, CapCut, After Effects, Ableton, FL Studio, Reaper, and Vegas.
07

System & Platforms

Multiplatform Support
Full feature parity across Windows, macOS (Intel and Apple Silicon), and Linux.
Customizable Shortcuts
Customize hotkeys for opening QuickSearch Overlay and controlling playback.
Advanced Settings
Settings to adjust default folders, indexing behavior, and editor preferences.
Lightweight Background Engine
Runs in the background with low CPU and RAM usage, so your video editor keeps full performance.
